> Trigger: When this folder's structure/responsibilities/file list changes, update this document.

Pages expose singleton admin surfaces that are not CRUD resources. / 页面暴露不属于 CRUD 资源的单例后台界面。
They may navigate or persist configuration but must not own registration rules. / 它们可以导航或持久化配置，但不得拥有报名规则。
Uploaded public assets must be readable by the member H5 through `/storage`. / 上传的公开资源必须能被会员 H5 通过 `/storage` 读取。
